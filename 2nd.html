    • 🏫College Overview



    Meenakshi Sundararajan Engineering College (MSEC) is an autonomous engineering institution located in Kodambakkam, Chennai. The college provides quality education in engineering and technology while encouraging students to develop technical knowledge, creativity, innovation, and practical skills through projects, internships, research, hackathons, and various extracurricular activities
